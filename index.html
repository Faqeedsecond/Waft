Waft Privacy Policy

Waft: Share Files Nearby · Developer: FH Store · Effective 7 November 2026

Waft lets you send files directly from your phone to another phone nearby. This policy explains what Waft does with your information. In short: Waft has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

What you send

Files, photos, videos, apps, contacts and folders go only to the phone you choose, and only after the person on that phone accepts them. They travel directly between the two phones over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, and every transfer is encrypted. They are never uploaded to the internet or to us.

Files you receive are saved on your phone in the Downloads/Waft folder. You can delete them at any time.

What nearby phones can see

While Waft is open, or for the 10 minutes you choose "Everyone" in Receive, nearby phones running Waft can see your device name. You can change this name in Waft at any time. When you connect from the radar, both phones show a matching security code so you can check that you're connected to the right phone. A QR-code connection is secured by a one-time secret inside the code itself.

What stays on your phone

Waft keeps a transfer history (file names, sizes, the other phone's name and the time) and your settings (device name, visibility, theme). These are stored only on your phone and are not included in backups. You can remove history entries, or clear all of it, in the History screen. Uninstalling Waft deletes them.

Permissions

Nearby devices, Bluetooth and Wi-FiTo find nearby phones and create the direct link between them.
LocationAndroid requires this permission for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning on some versions. Waft never reads, stores or sends your location.
CameraAsked for only when you tap "Scan QR code", to read the other phone's code. No pictures are taken or stored.
ContactsAsked for only when you choose to send contacts. Only the contacts you pick are read and sent, as a vCard, to the phone you choose.
Install appsLets you install an app someone sent you. Android asks you to confirm every installation.
NotificationsTo show transfer progress while Waft is in the background.
InternetAndroid requires this for any network connection, including the private link between two phones in QR-code mode. Waft does not connect to the internet.

Google Play services

Waft finds and connects to nearby phones using Google's Nearby Connections, part of Google Play services on your phone. Google Play services is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Waft is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
